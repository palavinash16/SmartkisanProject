# Summer Moong (Zaid Moong) Cultivation Guide

## Introduction
Summer Moong (Green Gram) is an ideal 60-65 day short-duration pulse crop grown after wheat harvest in Northern & Central India. It fixes atmospheric nitrogen into the soil, reducing fertilizer needs for the subsequent Kharif paddy crop while generating high market revenue.

## Climate
- **Temperature:** 25°C to 40°C
- **Rainfall:** 600-750 mm (Sensitive to waterlogging)
- **Sunlight:** Requires clear sunny days during pod maturation.

## Land Preparation
1. Perform 1 deep ploughing followed by 2 cross harrowings.
2. Ensure fine tilth and proper leveling to prevent water stagnation.
3. Apply 4-5 tonnes/acre of well-decomposed FYM (Farm Yard Manure).

## Seed Rate
- **Seed Rate:** 8-10 kg per acre
- **Spacing:** 20-25 cm row-to-row and 7-10 cm plant-to-plant.

## Seed Treatment
- Treat seeds with **Thiram (2g/kg)** or **Carbendazim (2g/kg)** to protect against seed-borne fungal diseases.
- Inoculate treated seeds with **Rhizobium** and **PSB (Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria)** culture (200g each per 10 kg seeds) for enhanced nitrogen fixation.

## Sowing
- **Best Time:** April 5 to April 30 (immediately after wheat harvest).
- **Depth:** 3-4 cm deep in moist soil.

## Irrigation
- **Total Irrigations:** 3 to 4 light irrigations.
- **1st Irrigation:** 20-25 days after sowing (Pre-flowering phase).
- **2nd Irrigation:** 35-40 days after sowing (Pod formation phase).
- **Critical Note:** Avoid irrigation during peak flowering to prevent flower drop.

## Fertilizer
- **Basal Dose (per acre):**
  - Urea: 10-12 kg
  - Single Super Phosphate (SSP): 75-80 kg
  - Muriate of Potash (MoP): 12-15 kg
- Apply all fertilizers at the time of final land preparation or sowing.

## Weed Management
- Apply **Pendimethalin 30% EC** @ 1.0 litre/acre as pre-emergence spray within 48 hours of sowing in moist soil.
- Perform one hand weeding at 20-25 days if weeds persist.

## Disease Basics
- **Yellow Mosaic Virus (YMV):** Transmitted by whitefly. Control whitefly by spraying **Imidacloprid 17.8% SL** @ 0.5 ml/litre water.
- **Powdery Mildew:** White powdery growth on leaves. Spray **Wettable Sulphur 80% WP** @ 2g/litre.

## Harvesting
- Harvest when 80-85% of pods turn dark brown or black (usually 60-65 days after sowing).
- Pick pods in 2 synchronised pickings or harvest whole plants using a reaper/combine.

## Storage
- Dry seeds under sunlight until moisture content drops below 9-10%.
- Store in clean HDPE bags with Neem leaf powder to protect against pulse beetle (Bruchid).
