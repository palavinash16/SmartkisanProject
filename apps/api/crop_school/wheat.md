# Rabi Wheat Cultivation Guide

## Introduction
Wheat is the staple Rabi cereal crop in Northern India sown in November-December and harvested in April.

## Climate
- Cool winter temperature (10-25°C) during vegetative phase; bright sunny weather during grain filling.

## Land Preparation
- 2-3 deep ploughings followed by planking.

## Seed Rate
- 40 kg/acre (normal sowing), 50 kg/acre (late sowing).

## Seed Treatment
- Tebuconazole 2DS @ 1.25g/kg.

## Sowing
- Sowing in lines 20-22.5 cm apart at 4-5 cm depth.

## Irrigation
- Crown Root Initiation (CRI) at 21 days is the most critical stage. Total 4-5 irrigations.

## Fertilizer
- N:P:K @ 50:25:15 kg/acre. Half N + full P & K at sowing; remaining N at 1st irrigation.

## Weed Management
- Sulfosulfuron 75% WG @ 13.5g/acre at 30-35 DAS.

## Disease Basics
- **Yellow Rust:** Spray Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1ml/litre water.

## Harvesting
- April harvest when grains become hard and straw turns golden yellow.

## Storage
- Store at < 12% moisture in dry godowns.
