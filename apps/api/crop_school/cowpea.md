# Cowpea (Lobia) Cultivation Guide

## Introduction
Cowpea is a versatile 50-60 day quick-growing legume grown for pods, grain, and green manure during the summer gap period. It excels in low fertility soils.

## Climate
- **Temperature:** 20°C to 35°C
- Drought tolerant, performs well in sandy to loamy soils.

## Land Preparation
- 2 ploughings to obtain medium tilth.

## Seed Rate
- **Grain Purpose:** 8-10 kg/acre
- **Fodder/Green Manure:** 12-15 kg/acre

## Seed Treatment
- Rhizobium inoculation + Thiram 2.5g/kg.

## Sowing
- Sow in April-May in rows spaced 30 cm apart.

## Irrigation
- 2-3 light irrigations at 15-day intervals.

## Fertilizer
- Apply 10 kg Nitrogen and 20 kg Phosphorus per acre at sowing.

## Weed Management
- One weeding at 20 days after sowing.

## Disease Basics
- **Mosaic Virus:** Vector control using Neem oil 1500 ppm @ 3ml/litre.

## Harvesting
- Tender pods for vegetable: 45-50 days; Grain maturity: 55-60 days.

## Storage
- Moisture level < 10% in sealed storage containers.
