# Summer Sesame (Til) Cultivation Guide

## Introduction
Summer Sesame is a high-value oilseed crop maturing in 75-80 days with exceptionally low water requirement and high market returns per quintal.

## Climate
- **Temperature:** 25°C to 37°C
- Prefers warm dry weather with assured light irrigation.

## Land Preparation
- Requires very fine seedbed since seeds are extremely small.

## Seed Rate
- **Seed Rate:** 1.5 - 2.0 kg per acre (mix with fine sand for uniform broadcasting/sowing).

## Seed Treatment
- Carbendazim 2g/kg seed.

## Sowing
- Line sowing at 30 cm x 10 cm spacing.

## Irrigation
- 3 irrigations: Sowing, Flowering, Capsule formation.

## Fertilizer
- 15 kg N + 10 kg P2O5 per acre.

## Weed Management
- Hand weeding at 15 and 30 days.

## Disease Basics
- **Phyllody:** Transmitted by leafhoppers. Spray Dimethoate 30% EC @ 1.5 ml/litre.

## Harvesting
- Harvest when leaves and capsules turn pale yellow (75-80 days).

## Storage
- Dry capsules, thresh, and store clean seeds at < 8% moisture.
