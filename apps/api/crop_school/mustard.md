# Rabi Mustard Cultivation Guide

## Introduction
Mustard is an important oilseed crop harvested in March-April, vacating fields early for short summer gap crops.

## Climate
- Cool dry climate (15-25°C).

## Land Preparation
- Fine seedbed with good moisture conservation.

## Seed Rate
- 1.5 - 2.0 kg/acre.

## Seed Treatment
- Metalaxyl 35% WS @ 6g/kg seed.

## Sowing
- October-November sowing in 45 cm spaced lines.

## Irrigation
- 2 irrigations: 30 days (flowering) and 60 days (pod filling).

## Fertilizer
- N:P:K:S @ 32:16:16:10 kg/acre.

## Weed Management
- Pre-emergence Pendimethalin.

## Disease Basics
- **White Rust & Aphids:** Spray Dimethoate for aphids; Mancozeb for white rust.

## Harvesting
- March harvest when pods turn bright yellow.

## Storage
- Store clean seed at < 8% moisture.
