# Summer Urad (Black Gram) Cultivation Guide

## Introduction
Summer Urad is a lucrative 65-70 day pulse crop that fits perfectly in the gap window between Rabi wheat and Kharif paddy. It significantly restores soil organic carbon and nitrogen reserves.

## Climate
- **Temperature:** 27°C to 38°C
- **Rainfall:** Warm climate crop, tolerates moderate dry spells.

## Land Preparation
1. Prepare a well-drained seedbed with fine tilth.
2. Incorporate 4 tonnes/acre FYM.

## Seed Rate
- **Seed Rate:** 8-10 kg per acre
- **Spacing:** 25 cm x 10 cm

## Seed Treatment
- Treat seeds with **Trichoderma viride (4g/kg)** and Rhizobium culture.

## Sowing
- **Best Window:** April 10 to April 25.
- Sow using seed drill at 3-4 cm depth.

## Irrigation
- Requires 3 irrigations at critical stages: Branching (20 DAS), Flowering (35 DAS), and Pod development (50 DAS).

## Fertilizer
- N:P:K @ 8:16:8 kg/acre as basal dose.

## Weed Management
- Pre-emergence Pendimethalin @ 1 litre/acre in 200 litres water.

## Disease Basics
- **Cercospora Leaf Spot:** Spray Carbendazim 50% WP @ 1g/litre at first appearance.

## Harvesting
- Pods turn black upon maturity. Harvest at 65-70 days.

## Storage
- Dry to 9% moisture; store in airtight bins with 1% vegetable oil seed coating.
